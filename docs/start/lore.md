# The Story of DewBot

## What is DewBot?

DewBot is the AI messaging gateway engine for [Dewx](https://dewx.ai). It connects multiple messaging channels through a single WebSocket gateway, with multi-tenant org isolation and Dew AI routing.

## The Vision

DewBot is built on the idea that AI assistants should meet you where you already are -- on WhatsApp, Telegram, Slack, Discord, and every other channel your team uses. No new apps to install, no new habits to form.

## Key Principles

- **Channel-native**: Your AI assistant lives in the channels you already use
- **Multi-tenant**: Built for organizations with proper isolation
- **Open**: MIT-licensed and extensible
- **Self-hosted**: Run it on your own infrastructure

## The Name

**Dew** -- fresh, clean, appearing naturally every morning. Like dew on grass, DewBot is the AI layer that appears naturally across all your communication channels.

**Bot** -- because that is exactly what it is: a bot that works for you.
